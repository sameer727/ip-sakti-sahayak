About IP-SAKTI Sahayak

A source-cited assistant for Ayurvedic intellectual property and regulatory guidance — across national and international regimes, in English and Hindi.

IP-SAKTI Sahayak helps practitioners, AYUSH startups, MSMEs and researchers navigate the overlapping regimes that decide whether an Ayurvedic product can be protected and commercialised: patents, geographical indications, trademarks, the Biological Diversity Act's access-and-benefit-sharing duties, and the drug / food / cosmetic regulatory tracks.

What the system does

  • Formulation Classifier — a deterministic, rule-based questionnaire sorts a product into Classical, Proprietary, Phytopharmaceutical, Ayurveda-Aahar, Cosmetic, New Drug — or Uncertain, with a clarification round.
  • India IP & Regulatory guidance — grounded answers with citations from real Indian statutes and registries (Patents Act § 3(p), GI Act, D&C Act, FSSAI Ayurveda-Aahara and more).
  • ABS / Traditional Knowledge guidance — Biological Diversity Act and NBA routes for plant-based products, with a safe TKDL pointer (TKDL's contents are restricted; the assistant points, never quotes).
  • International IP guidance — TRIPS, WIPO GRATK, and the filing systems kept strictly separate: PCT for patents, Madrid for trademarks, Hague for industrial designs.
  • Central assistant — routes each question to the right specialist and assembles the final cited answer; when evidence is insufficient it abstains instead of guessing.

Safety principles

  • Never invents legal authority, citations, URLs, fees or deadlines.
  • India and International guidance are never conflated — the jurisdiction toggle is authoritative.
  • Every answer carries citations from curated authoritative sources (India Code, IP India, NBA, CDSCO, FSSAI, WIPO, WTO).
  • Insufficient evidence is declined openly — abstention is a normal outcome, not an error.
  • Output is general information, not legal advice.

How it is built

A retrieval-augmented pipeline over a small curated corpus of real sources. Each specialist domain validates its own citations and confidence; a central assistant routes and assembles. Without a hosted-LLM key the system runs in a deterministic evidence-only mode — answers quote the retrieved provisions rather than inventing narrative.

Help

Asking a question

Pick a jurisdiction and language in the top bar, then ask in plain language — or start from a Quick demo scenario card. Follow-up questions work: keep them short and the assistant resolves them against the previous turn.

Jurisdiction

India answers Indian law: Patents Act, GI Act, Trade Marks, D&C Act, FSSAI, Biological Diversity Act. International answers treaties and routes: TRIPS, WIPO GRATK, PCT, Madrid, Hague. The two answer-sets are never conflated — the toggle is authoritative.

System reminder: PCT → patents · Madrid → trademarks · Hague → industrial designs.

Confidence & abstention

Every answer carries a confidence level (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW) computed from evidence strength. When the curated corpus cannot support an answer, the assistant declines openly — you will see an "insufficient verified evidence" state with the reason. That is a safety feature, not an error.

Formulation Classifier

Open the Formulation Classifier and answer the guided questions (in English or Hindi). The classifier is deterministic — the same answers always produce the same category — and incomplete or contradictory answers yield Uncertain with a clarification prompt instead of a guess.

Sources

Citations link to official portals — India Code, IP India, the National Biodiversity Authority, CDSCO, FSSAI, WIPO and WTO. TKDL (tkdl.res.in) is referenced as a pointer: its detailed contents are available to patent offices, not the public.

Limitations

This system provides general information, not legal advice — verify with official sources or a qualified professional before acting. For complex edge-cases, use the "Escalate to Human Facilitator" button on any response card to connect with the AIIA-ICAINE IP facilitation desk.
